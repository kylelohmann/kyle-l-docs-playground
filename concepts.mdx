## Players

A **player** is a golfer who has connected their Arccos account to your app. Each player has a `handicap_index`; negative values are plus handicaps. Player IDs start with `plr_`.

## Rounds

A **round** is 18 holes on one course. Each round has a score, average driving distance, strokes gained, and hole-by-hole detail. That detail covers putts, fairway hit, green in regulation, and whether the player hit from a greenside bunker. Round IDs start with `rnd_`.

[List rounds](/api-reference/rounds/list-rounds) returns summaries without holes. [Get a round](/api-reference/rounds/get-a-round) returns the full detail.

## Stats

**Stats** are averages across a set of rounds. Players and cohorts share the same eight stats, so you can compare them directly:

| Stat | Better when | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scoring_average` | Lower | Average score, normalized to par 72 |
| `driving_distance_yds` | Higher | Average driving distance in yards |
| `fairways_hit_pct` | Higher | Tee shots on par 4s and 5s that finish in the fairway |
| `gir_pct` | Higher | Greens hit in regulation |
| `scrambling_pct` | Higher | Missed greens where the player still made par or better |
| `putts_per_round` | Lower | Total putts per round |
| `three_putt_pct` | Lower | Holes with three or more putts |
| `sand_save_pct` | Higher | Greenside bunker holes where the player made par or better |

## Strokes gained

**Strokes gained** measures how many strokes a player gains or loses per round against a baseline, split into four categories:

- **Off the tee**: tee shots on par 4s and par 5s
- **Approach**: shots into the green
- **Around the green**: chips, pitches, and bunker shots
- **Putting**: shots on the green

A positive number means the player beat the baseline. `total` is the sum of the four categories.

Strokes gained is more useful than raw stats because it shows *where* strokes are lost. For example, a low fairways percentage may cost little if the player's misses are long and playable.

### Baselines

The API stores strokes gained against the **scratch** cohort. To measure against another cohort, subtract that cohort's strokes gained:

```text
strokes gained vs cohort = strokes gained vs scratch − cohort's strokes gained vs scratch
```

The [strokes gained](/api-reference/performance/get-strokes-gained) and [compare](/api-reference/performance/compare-a-player-with-a-cohort) endpoints do this for you.

## Benchmark cohorts

A **cohort** is a group of Arccos golfers at a similar skill level. Each cohort publishes its average stats and strokes gained.

| ID | Name | Handicap index |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tour_elite` | Tour elite | +2 or better |
| `scratch` | Scratch | +1.9 to 1.9 |
| `hcp_5` | 5 handicap | 2.0 to 7.4 |
| `hcp_10` | 10 handicap | 7.5 to 12.4 |
| `hcp_15` | 15 handicap | 12.5 to 17.4 |
| `hcp_20` | 20 handicap | 17.5 or higher |

**Tour elite** is the closest proxy for tour-level play. It's built from Arccos golfers with elite handicaps, not from tour event results.

<Note>
  Cohort numbers in the demo are illustrative sample values. See [Production checklist](/build/production) for how to source real ones.
</Note>

### Percentiles

For each stat, the compare endpoint returns a `cohort_percentile` from 1 to 99. It estimates where the player's value would rank inside the cohort, so 50 means the cohort's median. The demo assumes each stat is normally distributed around the cohort average, with a fixed spread per stat.

## Windows

Stats endpoints take a `window` parameter that controls which rounds count:

| Value | Rounds included |
| --- | --- |
| `last_5` | 5 most recent |
| `last_10` | 10 most recent |
| `last_20` | 20 most recent (default) |
| `all` | Every round |

Short windows show current form. Long windows smooth out one bad round.
